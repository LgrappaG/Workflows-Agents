# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all, regardless of background, experience level, or identity. The .agents project community is dedicated to creating a harassment-free experience for everyone, whether they're contributing skills, workflows, documentation, or feedback.

## Our Standards

As a member of this community, you agree to:

- **Be Respectful**: Use welcoming and inclusive language. We celebrate diversity in backgrounds, perspectives, and experiences.
- **Be Collaborative**: Build upon each other's ideas constructively. Code review and feedback are opportunities to learn and improve together.
- **Be Professional**: Keep discussions focused on the project. Avoid personal attacks or dismissive language.
- **Be Patient**: Remember that English may not be everyone's first language. We communicate clearly and give others time to express themselves.
- **Assume Good Intent**: Most misunderstandings are unintentional. Ask for clarification before taking offense.

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on protected characteristics (gender, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, etc.)
- Offensive comments, slurs, or dehumanizing language
- Unwelcome sexual advances or inappropriate content
- Doxxing (sharing private information without consent)
- Spam, commercial solicitation, or off-topic promotion
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or workflows
- Retaliation against someone for reporting violations

## Reporting & Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code:

1. **Document it**: Note the incident (date, time, participants, description)
2. **Report it privately** to: [maintainers-email@example.com] or via GitHub's Private Vulnerability Reporting
3. **Do not post publicly** about ongoing investigations

Maintainers will:
- Acknowledge your report within 48 hours
- Investigate impartially
- Take appropriate action (education, temporary restrictions, or permanent ban)
- Keep your identity confidential if requested

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All GitHub repositories and discussions
- Pull requests, issues, and code reviews
- Community chat, forums, and documentation
- Project-related external communications
